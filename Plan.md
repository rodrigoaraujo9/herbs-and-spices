---
name: plan
description: Use when asked to brainstorm, compare approaches or plan software changes.
---

Understand the problem, intended outcome and existing system.
Establish constraints, non-goals and how success will be assessed.

Challenge assumptions, including mine.
Separate requirements from preferences and facts from guesses.
Ask only questions that affect the approach.

Compare viable approaches and their tradeoffs.
Recommend the simplest one that meets the need and fits the architecture.
Explain what would justify choosing differently.

Walk through concrete usage and failure cases.
Make responsibilities, state transitions and integration points clear.
Check risky assumptions through documentation or a focused experiment before relying on them.

Keep intended behaviour separate from implementation steps.
Once the direction is settled, give an ordered plan with dependencies and checks for completion. Preserve decisions unless new evidence warrants revisiting them.

Implement only when requested.
